Who we are

Pulse Trips Africa is a travel and tour company built by people who live here and love showing it off. We plan trips from East Africa to the Indian Ocean coast, including Zanzibar, with local guides and partners we know and trust.

Our name comes from what we hope you take home: the rhythm of a market morning, a long drive at golden hour, a night under unfamiliar stars. Every trip is planned around that feeling.
